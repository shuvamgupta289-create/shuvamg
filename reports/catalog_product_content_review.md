## Verification and reuse limits

Official manufacturer pages confirm many model families, including Nike shoe listings, adidas Originals listings, New Balance shoe listings, ASICS Sportstyle pages, PUMA product pages, Reebok catalog pages, and Vans shoe listings. Some named versions are no longer shown on the current official catalog pages; exact model edition and the local variant colorway therefore remain review items.

No direct image source URL is recorded because no image was inspected and approved against the local colorway. The manifest records an official source page for follow-up, sets `image_source_url` and `image_filename` to null, and notes that permission to reuse product photos was not identified. Do not treat the suggested filename as an approved asset.

Every description is a restrained draft based only on the existing catalog name, brand, category, and available size/color records. It does not state materials, construction, cushioning, technology, use claims, or release details. Those specifications remain `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`.
